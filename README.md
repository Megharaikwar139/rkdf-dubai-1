### Key Features Included:
- **Concept 01 (Prestige Flagship):** Global Academic Premium design standards.
- **Hero Section:** Multi-slide panoramic Dubai skyline & campus aerial views with Ken Burns slow zoom-in effect.
- **Admissions Alert Box:** High-contrast solid obsidian glass with AED 15,000 grant details & 100% legibility.
- **Section 02 (Campus Portals & Services):** Dual-direction opposing auto-sliding infinite marquee rows (*Vice Versa*).
- **Section 03 (Heritage & Leadership):** Chancellor Shri Sunil Kapoor leadership spotlight + 2x2 interactive visual milestone photo cards (1995, 2011, 2017, 2026) with hover zoom.
- **Section 04 (Academic Architecture):** 11 professional schools horizontal auto-slider with hover-pause and infinite looping.
- **Section 09 (Research & Innovation):** High-contrast deep midnight canvas with glowing SVG neural network constellation nodes.
- **Section 14 (Institutional Credentials):** Continuous auto-sliding logo marquee of statutory charters (UGC, AICTE, NAAC 'A', PCI, BCI, Dubai KHDA, UAE MoE, AIU).
- **Bilingual Translation Engine:** Full English ⟷ Arabic (العربية) instant switcher with automatic `dir="rtl"` support.
- **Strict 2-Font Typography:** Cormorant Garamond (Headings) & Plus Jakarta Sans (Body).
