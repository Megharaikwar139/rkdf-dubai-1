03 · Heritage & Leadership

A four-decade legacy,
reimagined for the century ahead.

RKDF University brings together eleven professional schools, thirty-five academic departments and a cross-disciplinary research culture — under a single unwavering commitment to intellectual rigour and public good.

“An education worth having is one that makes you useful — to yourself, and to a world in motion.”

— Shri Sunil Kapoor, Chancellor

Founded by the Ram Krishna Dharmarth Foundation in 1994, our institutional network now educates over 25,000 students annually across medicine, engineering, management, and global sciences.

1995 · FOUNDING VISION

RKDF Educational Group Founded

Committed to accessible, quality technical and professional higher education across Central India.

2011 · STATE CHARTER

Established as RKDF University

Enacted under Madhya Pradesh Act No. 17 of 2011 with UGC Section 2(f) statutory approval and multi-faculty governance.

2017 · RESEARCH EXCELLENCE

50+ Research Labs & NAAC Accreditation

Commissioned state-of-the-art incubation centres, central instrumentation facilities, and funded DST/AICTE grants.

2024–2026 · INTERNATIONAL EXPANSION

Dubai Global Presence

Extending our academic footprint to the UAE, offering dual-campus opportunities, AI research studios, and global industry placements.
